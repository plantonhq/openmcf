# Trademark Policy

Everything in this repository is open source under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE): audit
it, fork it, build on it. The license itself draws exactly one boundary — its
section 6 grants no rights to trade names, trademarks, or product names. This
document explains what that boundary means in practice, as generously as
possible.

## The marks

The **Planton name** and the **Planton logo** are trademarks of
Planton Cloud, Inc.

The code is licensed to you; the identity is what tells people something comes
from, or is endorsed by, Planton Cloud, Inc. This policy protects that signal —
for you as much as for us, because it is what makes "built on Planton" mean
something.

## What you can do without asking

- **Say true things.** "Built on Planton", "compatible with Planton",
  "deploys with the Planton catalog", "a fork of Planton" — truthful,
  descriptive references to the project need no permission, ever.
- **Write and teach.** Use the name and logo in articles, talks, tutorials,
  courses, screenshots, and reviews that refer to the project.
- **Fork and build.** Fork this repository, modify it, embed its components,
  redistribute them — everything Apache-2.0 permits — and describe your work
  truthfully as derived from Planton.
- **Redistribute unmodified releases** and use the name to identify what they
  are.

## What needs permission first

- Using "Planton" — or anything confusingly similar — in the name of your own
  product, service, company, or domain, or in your own logo.
- Presenting a modified version, fork, or distribution in a way that suggests
  it **is** Planton or is endorsed, certified, or maintained by
  Planton Cloud, Inc.
- Altering the Planton logo or combining it with your own marks.

## Forks, specifically

The code is yours to fork — the license guarantees that and this policy cannot
and does not narrow it. The name is not part of the fork. A fork distributed as
its own product needs its own name and logo; calling it "a fork of Planton" in
its description is always fine.

## Questions and permission requests

Write to trademarks@planton.dev. We answer quickly and say yes to anything
that does not confuse people about origin or endorsement.
