# Certificate expiry warning

The don't-let-the-site-break shape: Universal SSL certificate events emailed to the platform team and mirrored into chat through a webhook destination. Cloudflare offers five certificate-expiry alert families -- `universal_ssl_event_type` here, plus `custom_ssl_certificate_event_type`, `dedicated_ssl_certificate_event_type`, `mtls_certificate_store_certificate_expiration_type`, and the two Authenticated Origin Pulls variants -- so clone this shape per certificate kind your account actually uses.
