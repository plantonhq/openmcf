## Traction

### Paying Customers

We have active revenue from IT consulting firms in India. These firms deploy client workloads to client cloud environments—a perfect validation of Planton's core value proposition.

### Active US Pipeline

We're in active conversations with two established American consulting companies:

- **Indiana**: 20+ year established consulting firm, demo completed, interest expressed
- **San Jose**: Well-established consulting firm, demo completed, working on onboarding

Both are American Native consulting companies with proven track records serving mid-market clients.

### Why IT Consulting Companies?

This isn't accidental. IT consulting companies are our ideal early adopters because:

- They **can't use PaaS** solutions (Railway, Render) — they deploy to client cloud environments
- They **can't justify Harness pricing** ($100K+/year) for mid-market client engagements  
- They **need repeatable, auditable deployments** across multiple client environments
- Planton fills this gap perfectly

## Use of Funds

Here's exactly how we're allocating capital:

### Product Development (60%)

- **Self-hosting**: Making enterprise deployment seamless and secure
- **End-to-end security**: Meeting enterprise compliance requirements
- **SOC 2 preparation**: Required for mid-market enterprise deals

We're not building features for feature's sake. Everything is driven by what our pipeline customers need to sign contracts.

### Payroll (25%)

- Lean team of 3, focused on shipping
- No bloat, no premature hiring
- Every person is a force multiplier

### Operations (15%)

- **Cursor, Claude, and LLM tooling**: This has become a significant part of our spend—and worth every dollar. We're accomplishing 10x more than we could otherwise.
- **Real estate**: Minimal
- **Standard SaaS subscriptions**: GitHub, monitoring, etc.

## What We're NOT Doing

**Not heavy GTM spend.** We have a strategic advisor helping source mid-market leads. We're not burning cash on paid acquisition or building a sales team prematurely.

Focus is singular: make the product bulletproof for the customers already in our pipeline.

## February Priorities

1. **Close current pipeline** → Convert demos to signed contracts
2. **Self-hosting hardening** → Enterprise-ready deployment
3. **First angel investments** → Extend runway to 18+ months
